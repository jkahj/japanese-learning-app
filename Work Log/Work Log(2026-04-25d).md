## How the Pitch Display Works

The existing `renderPitch(hiragana, n)` renders a row of morae with colored bars:
- Yellow bar = HIGH (`ph-hi`)  
- Dim bar = LOW (`ph-lo`)
- Label shows `[n] 平板型 / 頭高型 / 中高型`

Accent number rules:
- `n=0` → 平板型: first mora low, all others high
- `n=1` → 頭高型: first mora high, all others low
- `n≥2` → 中高型: mora 2..n high, rest low
